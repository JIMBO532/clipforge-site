ClipForge

ClipForge is a personal desktop tool that runs on its owner's own computer. It cuts short vertical clips from long gaming videos and streams, adds captions and a title, and posts the finished clips to the owner's own social media accounts.

It is used by one person to manage their own accounts. It is not a public service and has no sign-up.

How it uses TikTok

ClipForge uses TikTok's Login Kit so its owner can connect their own TikTok account, and the Content Posting API to upload videos the owner has reviewed and approved to that account. It does not post anything that has not been approved first.

Clips are made only from creators who allow clipping of their content, and each clip credits its source.
