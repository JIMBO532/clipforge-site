Privacy Policy

ClipForge is a personal desktop tool. This page explains what information it handles when a TikTok account is connected.

What is collected

When you connect a TikTok account, ClipForge receives through TikTok's API: your basic profile information (display name, username and avatar) and an access token that lets it upload videos to your account. It also receives the status of videos it uploads.

ClipForge does not receive your TikTok password, your messages, or your followers' information.

How it is used

The information is used for one purpose: uploading videos you have approved to your own account, and showing you whether each upload succeeded.

Where it is stored

Clip files, captions and posting history are stored on the owner's own computer. The account connection and access token are held by Composio (composio.dev), the service ClipForge uses to talk to TikTok. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept

The connection is kept until you disconnect the account. Posting history stays on the owner's computer until they delete it.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time in ClipForge, or in the TikTok app under Settings, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. To ask for stored information about your account to be deleted, use the contact address below.

Children

ClipForge is not intended for anyone under 18.
